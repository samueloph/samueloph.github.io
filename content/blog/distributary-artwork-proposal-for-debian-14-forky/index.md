In hydrology, a *distributary* is a channel that forks away from a river and
flows on to the sea, the opposite of a tributary. Debian is the river much of
the Linux world forks from, so for a release called forky, that is my proposal
for its artwork: one river, many forks.

All generated by code. A small Python program grows the river by the
rules of real deltas, and renders every wallpaper, login screen, boot splash
and GRUB background from it, dark and light, at any resolution: the showcase
offers 51 sizes to download, from phones to 8K.

It comes in eight colours, and with the [palette
editor](https://samueloph.dev/debian-distributary-theme/editor/) you can fork
your own: change any colour or option live in your browser, or roll random
ones, then download the wallpaper or build the whole theme locally. Have a look
at the [showcase](https://samueloph.dev/debian-distributary-theme/) and at the
[proposal on the Debian
wiki](https://wiki.debian.org/DebianArt/Themes/Distributary); the source is on
[Salsa](https://salsa.debian.org/samueloph/distributary) and
[GitHub](https://github.com/samueloph/distributary).
