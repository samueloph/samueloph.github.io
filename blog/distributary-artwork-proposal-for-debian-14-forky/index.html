Distributary: my artwork proposal for Debian 14 "forky"

Samuel Henrique (samueloph) October 31, 2026 [debian, artwork]

In hydrology, a distributary is a channel that forks away from a river and flows on to the sea, the opposite of a tributary. Debian is the river much of the Linux world forks from, so for a release called forky, that is my proposal for its artwork: one river, many forks.

All generated by code. A small Python program grows the river by the rules of real deltas, and renders every wallpaper, login screen, boot splash and GRUB background from it, dark and light, at any resolution: the showcase offers 51 sizes to download, from phones to 8K.

It comes in eight colours, and with the palette editor you can fork your own: change any colour or option live in your browser, or roll random ones, then download the wallpaper or build the whole theme locally. Have a look at the showcase and at the proposal on the Debian wiki; the source is on Salsa and GitHub.
